problem: "Intake and index PVC pipe 'coral' game objects from loading zone, then score onto staged 'reef' scoring zones with varying heights. In addition, be able to manipulate and score spherical 'algae' game objects. The robot is only allowed to hold one of each game object at a time."
approach: "Using a large funnel to collect 'coral' game objects efficiently, and a staged linear elevator mechanism with a multi-use end effector to score and grab both 'coral' and 'algae' game objects. Our team decided on an architecture, then prototyped multiple possible design for each subsystem; the final designs were distributed among the design team members."
result: "Intake subsystem was able to collect game objects from various angles and speeds successfuly in competition. The end effector subsystem was able to deliver game objects to all four levels of the scoring zone. the robot ended both regional competitions as a semifinalist, and qualified for the World Championship."
lesson: "The intake and end effector subsystems went through major design changes from prototype to final product, and still were upgraded after to optimize for competitive play. I learned how to manage the engineering design process as an open loop instead of a linear timeline."
